Guideline 3.1.2(c) — Subscriptions

We have addressed the missing subscription disclosure and Terms of Use link as follows:

• App Store metadata: The App Description (or EULA field in App Information) includes a functional link to our Terms of Use. The Privacy Policy URL field contains our Privacy Policy. See APP_STORE_METADATA_3_1_2c.txt in our project for the exact URLs used.

• In-app purchase screen (Settings → Upgrade): The subscription sheet now displays:
  – Subscription title (e.g. One+ Monthly / One+ Annual), subscription length, and price (StoreKit localized price).
  – Functional links to Terms of Use and Privacy Policy.
  – Standard subscription disclosure text regarding billing, auto-renewal, and Account Settings.

The attached screen recording demonstrates the subscription screen on iPad (or iPhone), including tapping Terms of Use and Privacy Policy to confirm the links open correctly.
